A still (jpg/png) or a video from this camera. Pick a moment where the platform floor and the coach doors are easy to see.
STEP 1 · PLATFORM CORNERS 0/4
Click the 4 corners of the area where people wait for this train, in order around the edge:
  1. Platform edge (screen doors), one end
  2. Platform edge, the other end
  3. Back of the waiting area, behind corner 2
  4. Back of the waiting area, behind corner 1
Anyone whose feet are outside this shape — inside the train, behind the glass, on the stairs — is not counted.
STEP 2 · COACH SEAMS
Click on the floor where one coach ends and the next begins (the gap between two coaches' doors). One click per seam — the line is drawn across the platform for you. No seams = this camera sees a single coach.
STEP 3 · TRAIN STRIP 0/4 (TRAIN ARRIVE / LEAVE DETECTION)
A band of the train body seen through the upper glass — window tops and roof line, above people's heads. When the train moves, this band slides sideways; that's how the Control Room knows it arrived or left. Click in order:
  1. Top edge, corner-1 end
  2. Top edge, corner-2 end
  3. Bottom edge, under 2
  4. Bottom edge, under 1
Keep it on the train, not the station sign or the door frames, and long — the more of the train it covers, the surer the detection. Optional: without it the clip departs at its end, as before.
Reference stills — needed when your clip is made of CCTV stills taken seconds apart (the train is never seen moving). After saving the camera: load a frame from this camera with a train standing at the platform and press the first button; load one with no train and press the second.
STEP 4 · DOOR CROP 0/4 (TRAIN CLASSIFIER)
A box around 2–3 screen doors, full height: what the trained classifier looks at to tell train from no train. Pick doors people rarely block, ideally the far side of the frame from the stairs. Click in order:
  1. Top of the doors, corner-1 end
  2. Top of the doors, corner-2 end
  3. Bottom (floor line), under 2
  4. Bottom (floor line), under 1
Mark the same doors for every clip from the same physical camera.
